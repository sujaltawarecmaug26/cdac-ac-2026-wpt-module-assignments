A little about us

Good coffee, no rush.

Little Ember is an imaginary neighbourhood café for people who love a good cup and a calm place to reset. Our small-batch menu keeps things simple, fresh and comforting.

Our idea: fewer choices, better ingredients, and a welcoming seat for everyone.

How to order

  1. Choose your drink or bake from the menu.
  2. Visit the counter or call ahead for pickup.
  3. Pay at the counter and enjoy your break.
